What this is

A capsule is one file (.eddi) holding a book and its finished narration. Make it once on a computer that can keep up, then play it on any device, online or offline, with no voice engine needed.

1. Make a capsule (use a computer, such as your Mac)

2. Play a capsule (any device)

Choose a .eddi file. Once it has opened you can switch on aeroplane mode: nothing else is needed.

🎙 Try Piper voices in Eddi

Compare an alternative to Kokoro without changing your normal book reader. Piper generates speech on your device, rather than sending your book text to a speech server.

Choose a voice and press Generate. The first use downloads the voice model.

The first sample for each voice may download a substantial model and speech-support files. Models may be cached by your browser. Voice switching starts a fresh test session. This is an experimental browser integration; some older phones or browsers may not have enough memory.

When you find a voice you like, compare its sound and generation speed with Emma in Meet the Narrators. The main Eddi reader still uses Kokoro.

If this experiment cannot load on your device, you can also try Piper's independent browser demo.

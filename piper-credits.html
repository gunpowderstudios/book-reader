Piper voice credits

The original voice releases are credited below. E.D.D.i. serves unmodified copies of the Bryce Beattie models for browser compatibility. Reading text is processed on your device.

  • Alba — ALBA dataset, University of Edinburgh, CC BY 4.0. Fine-tuned from the Hi-Fi TTS base below. Alba model card.
  • Cori — Cori Samuel, LibriVox reader 92. Hi-Fi Multi-Speaker English TTS Dataset, NVIDIA Corporation, Bakhturina, Lavrukhin, Ginsburg and Zhang, CC BY 4.0. The same corpus underlies the base used by Alba, Cori and Jenny. Cori model card.
  • Jenny (Dioco) — Jenny (Dioco) TTS dataset and attribution terms. Fine-tuned from the Hi-Fi TTS base. Jenny model card.
  • Joe — OHF Voice datasets, fine-tuned from the rdh dataset. Both are CC0. Joe model card.

Piper voice credits. The additional voices are from Bryce Beattie’s Piper releases. Jenny (Dioco) uses the Jenny (Dioco) dataset. Clean 100 uses LibriTTS-R by Yuma Koizumi and co-authors, based on LibriTTS by Heiga Zen and co-authors, under CC BY 4.0. The other Bryce Beattie releases are listed as public domain. The models are unchanged.

Speech runs with ONNX Runtime Web 1.18.0 (MIT) and the Piper WASM build and build instructions, which use Piper phonemize and eSpeak NG (GPL). See Piper’s voice licensing notes for how model and dataset terms differ.

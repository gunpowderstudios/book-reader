📚 Free Books

Thousands of wonderful books can be read for free. Many older works are in the public domain, which usually means their copyright has expired. Others are shared with the copyright holder’s permission. You can legally download and enjoy books that are free to use in your country.

A small note: copyright rules vary between countries, and a new translation or edition may have its own copyright. Check the information on each book’s download page. These are independent websites; EPub Reader does not host their books.

☁ Take your book with you

Moving to another phone, tablet or computer? Take your book and your place in it with you. No account needed.

1. On this device

Open your book, then press Save Book & Progress. Choose where to save it, such as iCloud Drive, Dropbox or your Downloads folder. The filename includes the date and time, so you can easily find your latest copy.

2. On your other device

Open E.D.D.i., press Load Book & Progress, and choose the file you saved. Your book opens where you left off.

Tip: Send the saved file to your other device or keep it in a shared folder.

Copyright: Only transfer books you are legally allowed to copy, even if you bought them.

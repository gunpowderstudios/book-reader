About E.D.D.i.

E.D.D.i. is a free, open-source reading app from Gunpowder Studios. View the source code.

We want E.D.D.i. to bring classic books to as many people as possible — even on older devices, phones and tablets. That is why we use two different on-device AI narration engines: Kokoro for desktops and laptops, and the lighter KittenTTS model for mobiles and tablets. Kitten helps us offer narration on more modest hardware, although performance still depends on the device and browser.

Thank you to the creators! Desktop and laptop narration uses Kokoro and the Kokoro project. We are grateful to its developers and contributors. Explore Kokoro voices.

Mobile and tablet narration uses KittenTTS by KittenML, brought to the browser with the KittenTTS Web project. A big thank-you to the KittenML team and open-source contributors for helping us make E.D.D.i. more accessible. Mobile narration is still in beta as we improve compatibility and playback.

Please use this app only with books you own, works in the public domain, or material you are otherwise authorised to use. Copyright in books and other content remains with the respective authors, publishers and rights holders.

E.D.D.i. is provided free of charge.
